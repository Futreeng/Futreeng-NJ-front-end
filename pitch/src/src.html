About the council

The ATU New Jersey State Council brings together the Amalgamated Transit Union locals that represent NJ Transit bus workers across the state: operators, mechanics and the people who keep New Jersey moving. The council coordinates bargaining, legislative work in Trenton and member advocacy for all nine locals.

◆ Build the local Sample

Get involved

Every strong local is built the same way: members become supporters, supporters become activists, activists become stewards, stewards become officers. Pick the next rung.

01 Member Pay your dues, vote in elections, come to the monthly meeting at your local. You are here if you're reading this →
02 Supporter Sign up for the alert line, show up to rallies, wear your union pin on the job. Takes 30 seconds →
03 Activist Join a committee, help with phone banks, hand out leaflets at your garage, talk to new hires about the union. Two hours a month →
04 Steward Run for garage steward. Represent your crew in grievances. Training provided. Elected by your garage →
05 Officer Run for an office at your local or on the state council. Shape the next contract. Elections every 3 years →
I want to do more. Drop your number and your steward will reach out about the next rung. No commitment, no spam.

COPE & the Trenton fight

Transit funding, assault laws, pension protection: every one is decided in Trenton. ATU-COPE is how transit workers pay for a seat at that table. Dollar-per-check, member-controlled, no corporate money.

◆ Active campaign

Call your senator today

S-2140 (Operator Safety Act) raises assault on a transit worker to a second-degree offense and requires automatic 90-day paid leave. It's stuck in Senate Judiciary. One phone call per operator moves it.

"Hi, I'm [your name], a bus operator with ATU Local [your local]. I'm a constituent. I'm asking the senator to move S-2140, the Operator Safety Act, out of Judiciary this session. Transit workers are getting assaulted on the job and we need this bill to pass. Thank you."

Council governance

The ATU New Jersey State Council is a federation of nine locals, governed by elected officers and standing committees. Members elect delegates at each local. Delegates elect the executive board.
